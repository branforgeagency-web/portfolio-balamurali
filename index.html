Chapter 03 — Inside the Group

WHAT EACH COMPANY

Seven companies with seven different jobs — and one founder who built each of them to solve a problem he saw first-hand. Pick a company to look inside.

THOUGHTFLOWS

No:1 Medical Coding Academy

Where it all began. ThoughtFlows Medical Coding Academy trains graduates for careers in medical coding and healthcare revenue cycle management — the back-office work that keeps hospitals and insurers running around the world.

The academy was built on one idea: a student should walk out job-ready, not just certified. Classrooms are run by people who have worked in the industry, and every batch is taught with the same goal — a first job in healthcare.

What we do

  • Medical coding training for life-science and allied-health graduates
  • Certification preparation, taught by working professionals
  • Revenue cycle management (RCM) fundamentals
  • Interview preparation and placement support
  • Classroom batches across Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Kerala

Branches: Saravanampatti, Hopes and Gandhipuram in Coimbatore · Ameerpet and Dilsukhnagar in Hyderabad · Kerala.

THOUGHTWORTH

Healthcare Pvt Ltd

The group's healthcare services company. ThoughtWorth Healthcare manages the back-office work that keeps providers paid — coding, billing and revenue cycle operations, done accurately and on time.

It closes the loop that ThoughtFlows opens: people trained in the classroom put their skills to work on live healthcare accounts, under the same standards they learned.
